This repo is where I will store code, data and docs related to my experiments I am doing here at the MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology for my PhD.
This might also include experiments unrealted to my PhD. The projects here might eventually move to their own repo if needed.

The first order of business is to create a system to capture my day to day lab work (both wet lab and dry lab).
The source of truth should be captured in plain markdown files as much as possible. Only use specialized formats when absolutely necessary for implementing certain features.

The kind of wet lab experiments I perform are as follows:
- Look at the papers from The Buzz Baum lab here: https://www2.mrc-lmb.cam.ac.uk/groups/baum/publications/
- Jason chin lab: https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lcNi1RUAAAAJ&hl=en and https://constructive.bio/ncaa/
- Michael ELowitz lab: https://www.elowitz.caltech.edu/publications
- Paul Blainey lab: https://blainey.mit.edu/publications/

And for dry lab:
- AutoGo by Eric jang: https://evjang.com/2026/04/28/autogo.html#cover and code here: https://github.com/ericjang/autogo

And in general, experiments to understand machine learning models deeply. How they learn, what they learn, and can we make better model architecture, optimizers and learning algorithms.
