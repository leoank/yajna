# Fixation of marine samples for flow cytometry analysis
## Description

Protocol to fix marine samples for flow cytometry analysis of phtoplankton and bacteria with SYBR-Green.  Fix at least 2 samples per depth sampled and at least 6 to 10 depths per vertical profile  Reference Marie, D., Rigaut-Jalabert, F. & Vaulot, D. (2014). An improved protocol for flow cytometry analysis of phytoplankton cultures and natural samples. Cytometry. 85. p.pp. 962–968.

## Steps


None
1. Prefilter seawater sample onto 200 µm mesh 
2amount
2. Add 1.5 mL of prefiltered seawater sample to a 2 mL cryotube
3. Add Glutaraldehyde 
15amount 
reagents
4. Add Pluronic (facultative) 
2amount 
reagents
5. Vortex
6. Wait 10 min 
600s
7. Flash freeze in liquid nitrogen tank
