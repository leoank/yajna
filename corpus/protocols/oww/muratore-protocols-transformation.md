# Muratore:Protocols/Transformation
### Bacterial Transformation

1. 200 μL chemically- competent *E. coli* + 5 μL DNA
1. 30 min on ice
1. 30 sec @ 42°C
1. 2 min on ice
1. add 800 μL  SOC
1. 45 min @ 37°C
    1. spread 200 μL on rich-media plates
    1. &rarr; 37°C O/N (inverted)
    1. store remainder @ 4°C
        - spread remaining 800 μL on fresh plates if poor/no growth observed
        - otherwise, discard remainder after ~24h
